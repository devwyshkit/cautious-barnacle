## The Return Rate Advantage (The Business Case)

Traditional personalised e-commerce has a secret: *returns destroy margins.*

A customer receives a mug, claims "the text is too small," demands a refund. The product is unsellable.

**WyshKit's answer**: The customer approves a digital mockup *before* production begins. Once approved, the sale is final. Return rate for personalised products approaches zero.

Non-personalised products follow standard 24-hour return policy (damaged/wrong only).

This is the moat. This is why we can command premium pricing. This is the Fiverr model applied to physical goods.

## The Hook Model Applied (Nir Eyal, 2014)

The **Cashback Flywheel** is not just a loyalty feature — it's a full Hook Loop:

```
TRIGGER:   "Anniversary next week" + WyshKit push / Instagram ad
ACTION:    Open app → browse → add to cart (3 taps)
REWARD:    Preview mockup (variable, emotionally resonant)
           + Cashback credit (variable %)
INVEST:    Saved address + review + wallet balance
   ↓
INTERNAL TRIGGER:  Next occasion → user "remembers" WyshKit without external prompt
```

**Messaging law**: Surface the wallet balance on the home screen ("₹48 WyshKit Money — use today").
The investment must be visible. Hidden wallet = broken Hook.

## Peak-End Rule Applied (Kahneman, 1993)

People judge experiences by their **peak** (most intense moment) and their **end**.

| Moment | What it is | What it must feel like |
|---|---|---|
| **Peak** | Preview approval (Slide to Approve) | Full-bleed mockup, haptic on slide, instant animation |
| **End** | Delivery + cashback credit | Confetti (brief ≤2s), large cashback display, "Share your gift" prompt |

**Never** show a generic "Order Delivered" plain push only. The end matters. Make it memorable.

## Anti-Dark-Pattern Promise

We do **not** use dark patterns. Ever. Not even the "acceptable" ones.

- ❌ **No confirmshaming**: Decline CTAs say "No thanks" — never "No, I hate amazing gifts"
- ❌ **No hidden fees**: Delivery fee & platform fee shown before checkout, not revealed at final step
- ❌ **No urgency theatre**: "Only 3 left!" must reflect true `stock_quantity`. Never fabricated.
- ❌ **No forced continuity**: No auto-enroll into paid tiers
- ❌ **No disguised ads**: Promoted vendors carry a visible "Promoted" badge. Always.
- ❌ **No pre-checked opt-ins**: All checkboxes unchecked by default. Always.
