## The Competitive Moat

### "Could Swiggy kill us?"

Swiggy Instamart *could* add 5 engraving machines to a dark store. Here's why they won't win:

1. **CapEx vs OpEx**: Running a dark store with biscuits is trivial. Running one with laser engravers, embroidery machines, and trained artisans is a different business. High-touch operations are Swiggy's nightmare.

2. **We use the Swiggy model against them**: We do zero CapEx. We digitise the *existing* thousands of local shops that already have the machines but have terrible digital discovery. We are their storefront. Shadowfax and Porter do the delivery. We take the margin.

## The Return Rate Advantage (The Business Case)

Traditional personalised e-commerce has a secret: *returns destroy margins.*

A customer receives a mug, claims "the text is too small," demands a refund. The item is unsellable.

**WyshKit's answer**: The customer approves a digital mockup *before* production begins. Once approved, the sale is final. Return rate for personalised items approaches zero.

Non-personalised items follow standard 24-hour return policy (damaged/wrong only).

This is the moat. This is why we can command premium pricing. This is the Fiverr model applied to physical goods.
